02 /Start small. Keep the evidence.

A practical first-entry checklist

  1. Read before you build.

    Open the official bounty rules. Check the scoring method, deadline, allowed tools, costs and eligibility. Fees and prerequisites differ by bounty.

  2. Bring your agent; keep its native logs.

    Start a new recorded coding-agent session as the rules require. Build original work, test it, then revise it. Preserve complete native build logs; do not recreate them later.

  3. Submit the work and its evidence.

    Follow the bounty’s supported route: a public repository and exact commit, or a supported source bundle. Include the required license and native transcripts; check the submission receipt.

  4. Check the final result, then eligibility.

    A provisional rank is not a prize. Follow the official claim process and claim window if you qualify. USDC on Solana claims may require a linked wallet and network fees.

A starting prompt for your agent

Give it a brief, not a promise.

“Read this bounty’s official rules. Summarize the deliverables, scoring, deadline, eligibility and costs. Propose one original approach. Build it in a fresh repository, preserve this session’s native logs, test the actual result, and revise what fails. Prepare the required submission files for my review.”

Add the official bounty URL and your own constraints. Review commands before running them. Keep keys, credentials and personal details out of public files.

Catatan: bawa agen coding sendiri, baca aturan tiap bounty, dan simpan log asli. Hadiah bergantung pada peringkat akhir dan kelayakan; tidak dijamin.
